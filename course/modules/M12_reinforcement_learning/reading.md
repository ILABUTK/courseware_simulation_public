# M12 Reading Guide

**Module**: M12 — Reinforcement Learning for Simulation Environments

## Required Reading

1. Book Chapter 14: *Reinforcement Learning for Simulation Environments*
2. Appendix D: *Markov Chains and Markov Decision Processes*

## Recommended External Reading

1. Sutton and Barto (2018), Chapters 1 and 6
2. Stable-Baselines3 documentation on DQN and PPO
3. Gymnasium documentation for custom environments

## Before Class

Be prepared to answer:
- What makes a problem an MDP rather than a one-shot optimization problem?
- What should count as the state in a queueing or clinic-control problem?
- Why is reward design as important as the learning algorithm?

## After Class

Use the M12 notebooks to describe one decision in your project that could be reframed as a sequential policy problem. If the problem is really static, say so explicitly and explain why RL is not the right tool.
