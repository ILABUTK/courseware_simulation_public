# M09 Reading Guide

**Module**: M09 — Experimental Design and Scenario Comparison

## Required Reading

1. Book Chapter 11: *Experimental Design and Scenario Comparison*

## Recommended External Reading

1. Law (2015), Chapter 12 — "Comparing Alternative System Designs"; covers CRN, indifference-zone ranking-and-selection, and multiple comparisons; the most complete treatment in the simulation literature
2. Nelson (2016), Chapter 7 — "Comparing Systems via Simulation"; concise and modern; particularly good on the paired-t and indifference-zone perspectives
3. Montgomery (2017), *Design and Analysis of Experiments*, Chapters 3 and 5 — full factorial and 2^k designs; read before L30 if you have not seen ANOVA before; the simulation context modifies but does not replace these foundations

## Before Class

Be prepared to answer:
- Why do common random numbers reduce variance in paired comparisons? What must be true of the random number streams for CRN to work?
- In a 2^3 factorial design run in simulation, how many replications do you need per corner point to detect a main effect of size δ with 80% power?
- What is a metamodel, and when is it worth the extra effort to fit one?

## After Class

Your midterm project is assigned at the end of this module. Use the L30 notebook to plan your factorial experiment: identify the factors, levels, and expected interactions. Write a one-paragraph justification for your chosen design before you begin running replications.
