# M11 Reading Guide

**Module**: M11 — Simulation Optimization

## Required Reading

1. Book Chapter 13: *Simulation Optimization*
2. Revisit Chapter 11 sections on factorial design and metamodeling

## Recommended External Reading

1. Fu (2015): overview of simulation optimization methods and practical tradeoffs
2. Barton and Meckesheimer (2006): metamodel-based simulation optimization
3. Nelson (2021): statistical issues in selecting a final design under simulation noise

## Before Class

Be prepared to answer:
- Why is the smallest observed sample mean not necessarily the best design?
- When is a simple grid search better than a more sophisticated solver?
- What is the difference between using a simulation to estimate performance and using a simulation to search for the best decision?

## After Class

Use the two M11 notebooks to identify one optimization question from your own project that can be framed as a decision vector, feasible set, and stochastic objective.
