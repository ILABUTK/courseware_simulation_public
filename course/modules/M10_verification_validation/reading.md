# M10 Reading Guide

**Module**: M10 — Verification and Validation

## Required Reading

1. Book Chapter 12: *Verification and Validation*

## Recommended External Reading

1. Sargent (2013), "Verification and Validation of Simulation Models" — *Journal of Simulation* 7(1): 12–24; the most-cited V&V reference; covers the full taxonomy of validation techniques and when to apply each
2. Robinson (2014), Chapter 7 — "Verification and Validation"; practical guidance with worked examples; pairs well with L35 on face validity and expert review
3. Balci (1998), "Verification, Validation, and Testing" — in Banks (ed.), *Handbook of Simulation*; useful reference for the V&V checklist introduced in L36

## Before Class

Be prepared to answer:
- What is the difference between verification ("did we build the model right?") and validation ("did we build the right model?")?
- Name three extreme-condition tests that should pass for any queue model.
- What does it mean for a model to be "valid enough" for its intended purpose?

## After Class

The midterm project is due during this module window (L33–L34). Your project submission must include a V&V section that addresses: (1) at least two verification tests you ran, (2) at least one validation comparison against real or theoretical data, and (3) a statement of the model's intended scope of use.
