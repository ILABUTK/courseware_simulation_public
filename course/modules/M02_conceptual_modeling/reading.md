# M02 Reading Guide

**Module**: M02 — Systems Thinking and Conceptual Modeling

## Required Reading

1. Book Chapter 3: *Systems Thinking and Conceptual Modeling*

## Recommended External Reading

1. Robinson (2014), Chapter 3 — conceptual modeling framework: purpose, scope, assumptions, and level of detail
2. Robinson (2008), "Conceptual modelling for simulation: definition and requirements" — *Journal of the Operational Research Society* 59(3): 278–290; the canonical reference for what a conceptual model must contain
3. Pidd (2004), Chapter 5 — pitfalls in model scope decisions and the danger of over-modeling

## Before Class

Be prepared to answer:
- What is the difference between the real system and the conceptual model? Where does each live?
- How do you decide what to include and what to leave out of a model?
- Why should assumptions be documented even when they seem obvious?

## After Class

For the system you described in HW-01, write a one-page assumptions document using the template from L04. For each assumption, state the consequence if the assumption is violated. Identify the two assumptions you are least confident about.
