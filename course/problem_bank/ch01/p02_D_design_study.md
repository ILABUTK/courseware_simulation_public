# Ch. 1 — P02 (D) ★★ Design a Simulation Study

**Type**: Design | **Difficulty**: ★★ | **Chapter**: 1

---

A regional airport is considering adding a second security checkpoint lane to reduce passenger wait times during peak hours.

**Task**: Design a simulation study (do not build the model — describe the study).

Your design document must address:

1. **Objective statement**: What specific question will the simulation answer? State it precisely.
2. **Scope and boundaries**: What is inside and outside the model?
3. **Inputs**: What data would you need to collect, and from where?
4. **Outputs / performance measures**: What metrics will you report to the airport manager?
5. **Scenarios**: What system configurations will you compare?
6. **Assumptions**: List at least four simplifying assumptions and justify each.
7. **Validation plan**: How would you validate the model before using it for decisions?
8. **Communication plan**: Who is the audience? What format will the final report take?

*Hint*: Follow the 12-step simulation study process from Chapter 1.
