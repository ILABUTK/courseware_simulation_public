# Ch. 12 — P01 (C) ★ Verification and Validation Concepts

**Type**: Conceptual | **Difficulty**: ★ | **Chapter**: 12

---

Answer the following questions in complete sentences. Each answer should be 2–4 sentences.

**Tasks**

1. **Verification vs. Validation**: Define model verification and model validation. Give one example of a verification failure and one example of a validation failure that could occur in a hospital emergency department simulation.

2. **The V&V distinction in practice**: A modeler builds a SimPy queue simulation. She runs it for 10,000 time units and finds that the simulated mean wait is 4.7 minutes, while the theoretical M/M/1 formula gives 4.2 minutes. Is this a verification problem or a validation problem? What additional information would you need to decide?

3. **Extreme-condition tests**: List three extreme-condition tests that any queue simulation should pass. For each test, state: (a) what condition you set, (b) what output you expect, and (c) what a failure of this test tells you.

4. **Face validity**: What is face validity? Who performs a face validity check, and why can't the modeler perform it alone?

5. **When is a model valid enough?**: A simulation model of a hospital ward predicts average length of stay (LOS) within 5% of historical data for 8 of 10 patient types, but overestimates LOS by 25% for one rare but high-cost type. Is this model valid enough for: (a) planning routine staffing levels; (b) designing a new isolation protocol for the rare patient type? Justify your answers.

6. **Documentation requirement**: Why must model assumptions be documented even when they are obviously true? Give an example where an "obvious" assumption turned out to be false when the model was applied to a different facility.
